How that works

You play. AutoStream does the sorting.

Three steps, and the first one is playing.

  1. Play, or pick a recording

    Any video of your game works, even an old one. You do not need to have streamed.

  2. See what it found

    Every kill, clutch and close call, on one timeline. Watch any of them, untick the ones you don't want.

  3. Make clips, reels or a highlight

    Choose what you want made. Change anything you don't like in the editor afterwards.

How does AutoStream find my moments?

Want AutoStream to handle going live too?

You don't need this to make clips. But if you stream, AutoStream can do the boring part: open a game and it starts your stream on YouTube, Twitch or Kick, names it after the game, records it, and ends it when you stop playing.

Every stream starts private, with a 20-second countdown and a Cancel button, so nothing goes public by surprise.

Windows only. You set it up once, in about 15 minutes.

Set up automatic streaming

Your recordings stay yours.

  • Free and open sourceNo paid version, no trial. Anyone can read the code on GitHub.
  • No accountNothing to sign up for. Download it and use it.
  • Files stay on your PCRecordings, clips and reels are saved in your Videos folder. Nothing is uploaded, and AutoStream never deletes a recording.
  • No AutoStream serverThere is nothing of ours for your videos to go to.

It only goes online for what you ask of it: your stream goes to YouTube, Twitch or Kick if you turn streaming on, a song link you paste is fetched from YouTube, and Valorant match details come from the Riot game client on your own PC.
