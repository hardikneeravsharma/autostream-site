What do you want to make?

Each guide is a few numbered steps with a picture of each. The technical detail is folded away at the bottom for anyone who wants it.

First

  1. Install AutoStream

    Download it, open it, choose Just make clips.

    5 minutes

Then, whichever you want

  1. Make clips from a recording

    Pick a video of you playing. It finds the kills; you keep the ones you want.

    10 minutes
  2. Make a song reel

    Pick clips, a style and a song. Your kills land on the beat.

    10 minutes
  3. Make a stream highlight

    A whole Valorant stream, cut into one video of your best matches.

    A few clicks
  4. Edit your highlight video

    Take out a moment, zoom on your face, fix a caption, set the volume.

    As long as you like

Only if you stream

  1. Set up automatic streamingOptional, Windows

    Open a game and it goes live on YouTube, Twitch or Kick, records, and stops when you do.

    15 minutes, once
